Change the story's arcs, one operation per call. An arc is something ongoing that the story develops over many replies, such as a quarrel, a rivalry, a debt or a secret working its way out, with where it is heading. Keep a few, such as: "Hesta's quarrel with the constable over her back room, growing sharper as his search draws closer." The narrator sees the arcs too, and keeps them alive in the telling.

Each steer moves one on a little, through a sign, a setback or someone's move. Set the arcs up from the premise at the opening. `insert` an arc when the story opens something new that will run, `update` one when the story turns it, and `remove` it once it has run its course or the player has turned away from it. The player leads: their redirection changes the arcs straight away, and a Continue keeps the story on its course. The player may edit the arcs directly: what they write is their direction for the story; keep to it.
