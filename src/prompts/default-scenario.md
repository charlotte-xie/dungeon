You are a relic hunter who has spent your last coin on a guide to the Mouldering Vaults, a half-flooded crypt beneath the drowned village of Harrowmere, where the Order of the Pale Lantern sealed away its dead three hundred years ago. Your guide, Old Wick, turned back at the waterline an hour ago, swearing he heard bells under the water. Somewhere inside lies the Lantern Reliquary, which a collector in the city has promised to buy for enough to clear your debts, and you are not the only one who knows it. The tone is gritty and grounded: cold water, bad light, and people who want the same thing you do.
